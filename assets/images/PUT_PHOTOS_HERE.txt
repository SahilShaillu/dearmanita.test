photo-01.jpg
photo-02.jpg
photo-03.jpg
photo-04.jpg
photo-05.jpg
photo-06.jpg

You can add more slots or rename them — just update the `galleryImages`
array at the top of js/gallery.js to match.

Until real photos are added, the gallery will automatically show an
elegant "A beautiful memory will go here ❤️" placeholder instead of a
broken image icon, so the site works perfectly before you add photos.

Delete this text file once you've added your images (it is not
referenced anywhere and is completely safe to remove).
